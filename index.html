⚙️ Process

How the Recommendation Engine works

A 4-step AI pipeline that turns your profile into a personalized opportunity feed — with explainable reasoning at every step.

1
Build Your Profile
Add your skills, interests, education, career goals, and preferences in a 5-step onboarding wizard.
2
AI Profile Analysis
Our engine computes 6 weighted factors: skill match, interest alignment, career goal match, education eligibility, preference fit, and deadline urgency.
3
Ranked Recommendations
All 45+ opportunities are scored (0–100%) and sorted by match. Different profiles produce genuinely different ranked lists.
4
Explainable Reasons
Every recommendation explains exactly why it was matched — "You have Python ✓", "Aligns with your AI career goal ✓", "Remote matches your preference ✓".
